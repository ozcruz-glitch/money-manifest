Money Manifest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Money Manifest is a household budgeting app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. In short: the app only stores what it needs to run your budget, it never sells or shares your data for advertising, and you can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in details. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and a unique account identifier from that provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" option, we receive a private relay address instead of your real one. We never see or store your Apple or Google password.
  • Budget information you enter. Expenses, income, savings goals (including how each goal is split between household members), actual spending entries, categories, the names of household members you add, scenarios, and display preferences such as colours.
  • Bank account nicknames. If you nominate accounts for bills to be paid from or transferred to, the app stores the name you give each account and any optional details you choose to type in, along with whether you've ticked each bill as done. The app never connects to your bank, and you don't need to enter account numbers for this feature to work.
  • Bank statement imports. When you import a bank export file, it is read on your own device. Only the resulting spending entries (a description and an amount) are saved to your account. The original file is not uploaded or kept.

What we don't collect

  • No bank logins or direct connections to your bank accounts.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for tracking.
  • No biometric data. If you turn on Face ID or fingerprint unlock in the iPhone app, the check happens entirely on your phone using Apple's built-in system. The app only learns whether it succeeded, and nothing about your face or fingerprint is sent anywhere.
  • No advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking tools.

How your information is used

Your information is used only to provide the app's features: signing you in, storing your budget, and syncing it between the devices of the members of your household. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising or profiling.

Who can see your information

  • Your household. Budget data belongs to a household. Anyone you invite with your household's invite code can view and edit that household's data. Only share your invite code with people you want to have that access.
  • Service providers. Data is stored and processed by Supabase, our database and authentication provider, and sign-in is handled by Apple and Google. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.
  • Legal requirements. We would only disclose information if required to by law.

How your information is protected

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in a database protected by access rules that limit each household's data to its own members.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your sign-in and your membership of your household. If you are the last member of a household, the household and all of its budget data are deleted as well. If other members remain, the shared household data stays available to them.

You can also export a copy of your data at any time from Settings → Custom export.

Children

Money Manifest is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
